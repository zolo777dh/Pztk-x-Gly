NDP Party Cooldowns
===================
The party cooldown tracker of Naowh Defensive Planner as a small addon of its own. It works next to
any other addon (Naowh Smart Reminders, NSRT, ElvUI...). With Naowh Defensive Planner itself enabled
it turns off, because the full addon already has it (/ndp party).

Install: put the NDPPartyCooldowns folder into World of Warcraft\_retail_\Interface\AddOns and
restart the game.

It is on by default and shows the important cooldowns of every party member beside the Blizzard or
ElvUI party frames. Type /pcd in chat for the settings:
  /pcd on | off, /pcd frames | list, /pcd left | right | top | bottom, /pcd size 20-40,
  /pcd unlock | lock, /pcd ready, /pcd names, /pcd self, /pcd reset, /pcd report

Other players need nothing installed. Players with this addon or Naowh Defensive Planner also send
their exact cooldowns to the group out of combat. Cooldowns that the game shortens by what a player
does in combat (resource spent, charges consumed...) are shown only for yourself, because the game
hides that for others.
