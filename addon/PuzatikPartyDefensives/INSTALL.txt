Puzatik Party Defensives
========================
Tracks the defensive, interrupt and crowd control cooldowns of every party member. It works next to
any other addon (ElvUI, party frame addons, boss mods...).

Install: put the PuzatikPartyDefensives folder into World of Warcraft\_retail_\Interface\AddOns and
restart the game.

It is on by default and shows the important cooldowns of every party member beside the Blizzard or
ElvUI party frames. Interrupts and crowd control have movable lists of their own. Type /pcd in chat
for the settings window, or /pcd help for the chat commands:
  /pcd on | off, /pcd frames | list, /pcd left | right | top | bottom, /pcd size 20-40,
  /pcd unlock | lock, /pcd ready, /pcd names, /pcd self, /pcd reset, /pcd report

Language: Russian on a Russian game client, English on every other one. The LANGUAGE list at the
top of the window picks one by hand.

Other players need nothing installed. Players with this addon send their exact cooldowns to the
group out of combat. Cooldowns that the game shortens by what a player does in combat (resource
spent, charges consumed...) are shown only for yourself, because the game hides that for others.
