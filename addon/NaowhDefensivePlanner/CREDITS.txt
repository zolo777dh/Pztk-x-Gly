Naowh Defensive Planner: credits and sources

THANK YOU
This addon would not exist without the work of other addon authors. NDP is its own
code and does not include their addons, but it learned from them, reads the same
game events, and uses public facts about encounters that they made visible.

- BigWigs and LittleWigs, by Funkeh, Nebula, Justwait, Delk and the BigWigsMods team.
  The built-in raid timers and the Mythic+ catalog use encounter facts (spell IDs and
  timings) read from their public modules. NDP also listens to BigWigs events when it
  is installed.
- Deadly Boss Mods, by the DBM team, and EXBoss. NDP listens to their timer events
  when they are installed. The Mythic+ catalog uses facts (encounter and spell IDs,
  durations) from the EXBoss data files.
- Northern Sky Raid Tools, by Reloe and Rav. The note line format
  (time:;ph:;text:;tag:) and the boss ability categories follow NSRT, written anew
  from those facts. NDP reads NSRT export strings.
- Naowh Smart Reminders, by Naowh. The defensive reminder ideas and the old string
  format, which NDP still reads.
- Timeline Reminders, by Bart. The expected boss timelines on the Timeline page are
  facts (spell IDs and seconds) from its community timelines. NDP reads !TR2: strings.
- Mythic Dungeon Tools, by Nnoggie, and Raider.IO: dungeon, enemy and map IDs.
- Method guides were used only as names for reference; no guide text is included.

None of these addons' code is copied into NDP, and no protected or licensed data was
bypassed to read the facts above. Encounter facts can change with each patch; any
mistakes in them are ours, not the original authors'.
