Naowh Defensive Planner 0.9.0 — in-game editor and reminder engine
(formerly Puzatik Reminders / PztkReminders)

INSTALL
Close WoW completely. Copy the NaowhDefensivePlanner folder into Interface/AddOns.
New textures and fonts (logo, icons) only load after a full client
restart; /reload is not enough the first time.

UPGRADING FROM PUZATIK REMINDERS (PztkReminders 0.3.x)
The folder, SavedVariables and command changed. Keep your profiles one of two ways:
- Before updating, open /pztkr > Profiles > Export active, copy the PZTK1 string,
  then import it with /nwhdp > Profiles > Import profile; or
- With WoW closed, copy WTF/Account/<account>/SavedVariables/PztkReminders.lua to
  NaowhDefensivePlanner.lua in the same folder. The addon adopts it on first load.
Then delete Interface/AddOns/PztkReminders. Both addons must not run together;
this one pauses while PztkReminders is loaded. PZTK1 strings stay valid.
Disable NaowhSmartReminders, NorthernSkyRaidTools and TimelineReminders while
using this addon. It pauses reminder dispatch if those engines are loaded.

OPEN
Use /ndp or the minimap addon compartment menu (/nwhdp, /pztkr and /puzatik still
work). The window follows the design mockup: a top bar with the logo, search
(type, then press Enter), timeline / anchor / roster buttons, your specialization,
profile and boss mod status; a sidebar grouped as Planner (Overview, Mythic+, Raids,
Timeline), Raid (Notes, Raid tools, Players, Quality of life, Combat tools) and System (Profiles, Settings,
Diagnostics). Mythic+ and Raids each switch between Mechanics, Defensive sets and
Debuffs.

SPECIALIZATION
The window shows and edits only your current specialization. There is no picker:
switch spec in game and the window switches with it. Sets and reminders of other
specializations and other classes are not shown, and the editor refuses to change
them. "Copy this spec's setup to..." only offers specs of your own class.

LOOK AND FONT
The interface text is English. URW Gothic (Book / Demi) ships with the addon. The
Naowh font is commercial and is not included: to use your own licensed copy, put it
at Media/Fonts/Naowh.ttf, restart the game and pick it in Settings > Window > Font.
The window is static: no animated background, particles, cursor trail, flashes or
glows, and nothing redraws while it sits open.

REMINDERS ON SCREEN
A reminder is the spell icons in priority order plus their names, for example
"Barkskin and Survival Instincts", with no panel or bar. The names are spoken
(text to speech) unless Settings > Sound > Speak reminders is off. The called spell
glows on your Cooldown Manager and on Blizzard, Bartender, ElvUI or Dominos action
buttons; Settings > Reminders > Glow the called spell on your bars turns it off.
On the Defensive sets switch the gear of each ability sets its own text on screen, the
spoken phrase or a sound instead of speech, and whether it speaks at all; Hear it
plays it first. The last row, Call for an external, has its own phrase, voice and
an optional EXTERNAL! line in group chat.

RAID PAGES
Notes: shared notes (Load & send gives them to the group; leader or assistant only)
and personal notes, shown in a movable frame. Lines with your name or nickname are
your assignments and can pop up on pull ("Show my lines now" tests it).
Raid tools: ready check, pull and break timers, invite on whisper keyword, guild
invites by rank, group arrangement, automatic assistants.
Players: nicknames shared with the group and an addon version check.
Reminders start at a fixed left edge and grow to the right. The unlocked anchor
(/ndp anchors, or the anchor button) is a sample icon exactly where the first
reminder icon appears; drag it and lock it again.
Drag an ability from the spellbook or an action bar onto a defensive set to add it.
Lists and long text scroll with the mouse wheel. An open drop-down list closes on
a click anywhere outside it.

DEBUFFS (Mythic+ and Raids, next to Mechanics and Defensive sets)
A game sound when a debuff is applied, stacks or falls off (the game allows only
sounds here, not speech).
"Add racial debuffs" fills in the season dungeon debuffs (Mythic+) or raid boss
debuffs (Raids) that Stoneform or Shadowmeld can clear; the alert plays only
while the racial is ready. The raid list comes from the Encounter Journal (the
abilities it marks magic, curse, poison, disease or bleed) and is empty until the
journal has been read; it is not confirmed in game yet.

Navigation preserves your selected encounter, specialization, defensive-set draft,
reminder fields, settings draft and scroll position. Drafts are saved locally on
navigation, close and logout/reload, separately for each client/profile/spec.
Save set / Save reminder / Save settings activates the reviewed change. Exports
include saved profile content; incomplete editor drafts stay local.

MYTHIC+
1. Open Mythic+ (or Raids) > Defensive sets. Create a named set; click known defensives under
   "Add from your spellbook" or enter a spell ID. Set priority with the up / down buttons;
   Together combines checked abilities; On controls whether an ability is used.
   "Copy this spec's setup to..." replaces another spec's sets and reminders.
2. Open Mechanics. Select dungeon, NPC/boss and its ability.
3. In the reminder card choose the defensive set and the warning time (WHEN),
   then text, sound, duration and TTS (SHOW). The preview and the summary line
   show what will fire.
4. Save reminder. Test is a display preview; check the actual trigger in game.
M+ is restricted to Mythic+ difficulty. ExBoss supplies trash countdowns;
BigWigs/LittleWigs or DBM supplies boss messages and bars.
Built-in raid timers: without BigWigs or DBM the addon runs its own boss timers
for the ten raid bosses of the current tier, read from the game's encounter
timeline from the pull. They feed the same "Boss mod timer bar" and "Boss mod
message" triggers. Settings > Boss mod source: Automatic (a connected boss mod
first, otherwise built-in), Built-in raid timers (always), BigWigs or DBM.
Add timers (small adds) are not included. Not yet checked on live pulls.

RAIDS
Select raid, boss and Heroic or Mythic explicitly. Raid defensive sets are separate
from Mythic+ sets, even with matching names or keys. Bind a defensive set to a
mechanic or create a timed reminder from pull / phase. Actions include a chosen
spell icon, text, countdown, circle, sound and a supported player-frame glow.
Recipients can be the selected spec, role, player or everyone. Imported combined
recipient filters and difficulty filters can be retained. Save explicitly.
Non-defensive reminders have a LOOK section, like NSRT Encounter Alerts: text
colour, a countdown bar with its own colour and ticks (seconds left, e.g. 5, 10),
a custom icon spell ID for bars and circles, Show seconds left (bars and circles
hide their timer when it is off, text and icons add the seconds after the text),
Stay after timer (sticky seconds after the countdown ends) and Also glow players
(raid frames of the listed names, with a glow colour). Profiles that use these
need addon 0.6.5. Defensive call-outs keep the plain icons and names look.

QUALITY OF LIFE (/ndp qol)
A movable text display (font size, outline, Unlock and preview) shows short notices:
Gateway usable (when you carry a Gateway Control Shard), boss resetting / has reset
after a wipe, Loot the boss after a raid kill, and a Soulwell, Feast, Cauldron or
repair bot put down by the group (recognised by the English spell name, out of
combat). Ready check lists what you miss: raid buffs a class in the group can give,
a Vantus rune in raids, flask, food and durability under 30%; Check me now runs it
at once. Convenience: spell IDs in tooltips, auto-repair (optionally from the guild
bank) and accepting group invites from guild members. Options that are off register
no events.

RAID TOOLS ADDITIONS
Sorting: Default arrangement, Split groups (two halves with the same roles) and
Split evens/odds; Abort sorting stops it. Assistants: promote guild officers up to
a chosen rank, always promote everyone, automatically or now. Break timer: show the
bar, a sound at the end, print the time left each minute, unlock and preview to
move the bar, announce in group chat.

COMBAT TOOLS (/ndp combat)
Interrupt display: a kick order (typed, or the "Kick:" line of the shared note) with
the player to kick now and the next one coloured, number and name font sizes, width,
and a movable frame. It moves on when a listed player interrupts (your own casts
always count; the game may hide other players' casts in combat), and starts over
after combat or at a pull. Next kicker / Start over move it by hand.
Pace comparison: during raid pulls the boss health is sampled every few seconds
and the text display shows +/- percent against your best pull of that boss and
difficulty (the kill, or the lowest wipe). If the game hides boss health, it says so.
Private auras: big icons of your own private auras (and the other tank's, as tank),
drawn by the game into slots you can size and move.

EXPORT STRING (!NDP1:)
Profiles > Export all profiles gives one !NDP1: string with every profile of this
game client, Mythic+ and raid data together. Only active exports the selected
profile. Import adds every profile of the string (same names get a number) and
selects the one that was active. The format is the addon's own: CBOR, raw
Deflate and Base64. Older PZTK1 strings still import.

TIMELINE TOOLS
The Mechanics lane shows reminders and defensive sets tied to boss abilities at
the times those abilities happened on your recorded pull. + Timed reminder can
also make a "Boss mod timer bar" or "Boss mod message" reminder for a chosen
boss ability.
Before your first pull the Boss lane shows the "Boss (logs)" base: the median
cast times of public kills on Warcraft Logs, built by tools/build-wcl-timelines.mjs
into LogTimelines.lua. A boss without that base lists its abilities without times
(the spells the built-in timers announce, then the game's Encounter Journal). Left-click
one to plan it for every cast: Defensive set or Note (your own text, e.g. Stack in
middle). After a recorded pull the lane shows each cast in time, and a click plans
that cast: Defensive set, Note, Every cast or Timed here.
NDP needs no data from other reminder addons: raid bosses, ability names, icons
and categories come from the game's Encounter Journal, read out of combat when the
window opens (once per game build); times come from your own pulls. Export boss
also carries your recorded pulls of that boss, so a raid lead's export shows the
abilities in time for the whole raid before their first pull. Your own pull always
replaces a shared one.
Simulate pull: plays the boss timeline through your reminders outside combat, so you
see and hear every call as on the pull. A simulated pull records nothing.
Cooldowns lane: which spell of each planned defensive set will be ready at each call,
from cooldowns, charges and the set priority; a red ! marks a call with nothing ready.
Recipients also include Rotation by cast (names in order, or note:Tag to read the note
line "Tag: name name"), Position in a note line, Raid group and Class.
Boss health below: fires once per pull when a boss drops under the percent you set.
Aura on me: fires each time an aura with that spell ID lands on you. Health or auras
the game hides read as unknown, and the reminder stays quiet.
Last pull lane (Timeline): your casts of set spells on the last pull, and a red x on
each defensive call with no matching cast within 8 seconds. Raid / Mine switches
between the whole plan and only your reminders. Templates save named versions of a
boss plan (e.g. Prog, Farm); Apply replaces the plan and keeps the old one in History.
Import modes (Profiles): Add as new, Merge into the profile of the same name, or
Replace it. Export boss (Timeline) makes a string with only that boss; importing it
replaces only that boss in your active profile. History keeps the last 10 versions
from before a merge, replace, boss import or restore; Restore brings one back.
Zoom and scroll: Ctrl + mouse wheel zooms the timeline (30 s to 10 min), the
wheel alone or the slider under the axis moves it from 30 s before the pull to
the end of the fight. The - / + and 1m / 2m / 4m buttons do the same.
Boss timeline: each pull records when your boss mod announced the boss abilities.
The kill (or the longest pull) is kept, and the Timeline page shows it as a
"Boss" lane. Click an ability icon to start a reminder at that time. You need one
pull with BigWigs, DBM or the built-in raid timers before the lane appears.
NSRT-style note lines also work: time:90;ph:2;text:Soak;spellid:123;tag:Name Name;
glowunit:Name;dur:5. The time counts from the start of the phase; no tag means everyone.
Boss lane strips follow the Encounter Journal icons: tank (blue), interrupt (green),
dispel (purple: magic, curse, poison, disease, bleed), enrage (pink) and healer
alert (orange).
Note timers: lines like {time:1:30} Soak - Name in the shared or personal note
show as reminders on the pull. Who can be a name, {everyone}, class:, spec:,
role:, group: or type:melee / type:ranged. Turn them off or set how early they
show (4-10 seconds) on the Notes page.
Reminder card: "Melee or ranged" recipients, "Hide when I cast the spell" (icon,
bar and circle) and "Spoken countdown" (3, 2, 1 at the end of the timer).
Send to group (Timeline page, leader or assist) shares the plan for that boss and
difficulty. Received reminders are kept under the sender's name.

TIMELINE REMINDERS IMPORT
Profiles > Import profile reads Timeline Reminders exports (!TR2:, and the older
!TR:). Paste one or several lines; each is one boss and difficulty (Heroic or
Mythic; dungeon bosses become Mythic+ reminders). Reminders timed from the pull
keep their time and duration. Reminders timed from a spell event (cast start,
cast success, aura applied/removed) start from the boss mod message of that
spell and its count, so check those timings in game. Reminders timed from a unit
death, yell or emote are listed in the report and not imported. Text, icon, bar
and circle looks, colour, ticks, linger (stay after timer), text to speech, glow
names and recipients (everyone, name, role, class/spec, group; melee/ranged as a
list of specs) are kept. Sounds from the Timeline Reminders folder are left out.

PROFILES
Create, copy, rename, delete, select, import or export a profile inside the game.
!NDP1: is the native export (all profiles, M+ and raids). Imports create separate profiles.
Direct imports also accept unlicensed NSRPACK2 and current !NSRT:Profile: or
!NSRT:EncounterAlert: exports (full, single alert or group). Review the migration
report in the string box. NSRT glow units glow those players' raid frames next to
the reminder. Negative phase-1 times become "Before pull (countdown)" reminders:
they start from a /countdown or boss-mod pull timer, and the boss is the nearest
planned boss among the Encounter Journal pins on your map (/ndp diag shows it).
Profiles using either feature need Naowh Defensive Planner 0.3.3.
Original migration strings and reports stay locally in SavedVariables. Unavailable
conditions, displays and source engines are reported; they are not guessed.
Account-licensed NSR strings remain managed in NSR. Licenses are not bypassed.
Old shared sets get independent content copies on the first editor save. Existing
legacy root data is retained for compatibility; scoped sets are used by this engine.

BOSS ABILITY ALERTS
Timeline > click a boss ability > Note (or Every cast) creates a Boss ability alert:
the ability's icon and your text (e.g. "Tank Hit") with the seconds left, as in boss
mods. Style: Icon and text, Text only, or Bar with timer. These alerts stack on their
own anchor; /ndp anchors (or Display > Unlock anchor) shows both anchors to drag.
Any mechanic reminder can use the action "Boss ability alert".
Like NSR, a defensive on a boss ability can show two things at once: your personal
defensive on the reminder anchor and, on the ability anchor, a small ability icon
with your text (e.g. Empowering Slam + "Tank Hit") counting down to the cast. Turn it
on in the reminder card: TEXT ALERT (Show the boss ability with my text) and PERSONAL
DEFENSIVE (a set by priority, or SPELL = one spell of the set) work together. Choose
"Nothing else (text alert only)" for the text alone.

SETTINGS / DIAGNOSTICS
Configure icons, text, sizes, position, voice volume and boss-mod source.
Voice phrases: type your own text for each spoken cue (Use a defensive, Move out,
Dispel me, Stoneform, Shadowmeld); empty keeps the default. The game's own voice
speaks them. Default
spell labels and icons identify the chosen defensive, not only the boss ability.
Together callouts show the selected group. Public readiness chooses priorities;
closed/unknown charge counts are not guessed. Fixed spell-icon reminders do not
require readiness-based selection. Preview is not a live encounter test.
Use /ndp diag (or the Diagnostics page) for a copyable build/provider report.
The Display page previews a reminder and unlocks the anchor so you can drag it.

CLIENTS / DATA
Targets remain Retail 12.1.5.70448 and Forever 1.60.1.70425. Client workspaces are
separate. The bundled eight-dungeon catalog is Retail reference data from
LittleWigs, ExBoss and MDT IDs; only evidenced timer/message keys are offered for M+.
Raid bosses and their abilities come from the game's Encounter Journal; the raid
Stoneform list is the journal's magic, curse, poison, disease and bleed abilities.
Forever uses imported or manually added encounters.
Add mechanic accepts explicit map/NPC/encounter/spell IDs from your game client.
Provider packages are separate. The supplied ExBoss build requires EXBossData,
ExwindCore, EXBOSS-Locale and its required EXBOSS-LocaleBase dependency.

PULL TRACE (Diagnostics, /ndp trace)
Every pull is traced: each boss mod timer bar and message with its time from the
pull, each phase change, and every reminder with what it did: shown, or skipped
and why (turned off, other difficulty, not for you, already covered, no spell of
the set ready, paused by another reminder addon). The last three pulls are kept.
One wipe is enough to see which call did not come. Pull trace, Observed and Your
calls on the Diagnostics page put the reports in the copyable box; /ndp trace off
stops it. /ndp keys lists the spell IDs the boss mod used, marked "planned" when a
reminder of yours uses that ID.

STARTER SET
Defensive sets > Starter set fills in the catalog's personal defensives your
character knows, the short and frequent ones first (Barkskin, Feint, Ice Barrier...)
and the big ones and immunities last. Externals and raid cooldowns are left out.
Review it and Save set. /ndp starter saves one for Mythic+ and raids where that
content has no set yet.

INVITE AND ARRANGE FROM THE NOTE
The shared note can list the raid: "Group 1: Name Name Name" (or "G1:") up to
group 8, and "Invite: Name Name" for extras. Raid tools > Invite from note invites
everyone listed who is not in the group yet; Arrange from note moves the listed
players into their groups and leaves the rest where they are. Nicknames work.

NICKNAMES ON UNIT FRAMES
Players > Show nicknames on Blizzard party and raid frames. ElvUI: the tag
[ndp:nick]. Other addons and WeakAuras can call NDPAPI:GetName(unit or name).

PARTY COOLDOWNS
Raid > Party cooldowns (/ndp party). Tracks the important cooldowns of every
specialization (personal defensives, externals, raid cooldowns, burst) for you
and your party, or your own group in a raid. Timers start from real casts, in
and out of combat. Shown as a movable list or beside the Blizzard / ElvUI party
frames. Abilities: switch any spell of a class off, or add your own by spell ID.
Lengths are the game's base cooldowns; players running this addon send, out of
combat, exactly which of these spells they know and their real cooldowns (also
the 1 min+ ones from their Cooldown Manager), so talents count. For other players the spec and
talents are read by inspecting them out of combat; talents they did not take
are left out. In combat the
game can hide other players' spells: such casts are counted as hidden on the
page and nothing is guessed. Mythic+ keys and raid encounters reset all timers.

LIMITS
No combat or active-encounter editing. No automatic ability presses. Native aura
sounds need the corresponding public game API. Several source behaviors remain
unavailable; imports retain and report them. Dungeon reminders run in every
dungeon difficulty (Normal, Heroic, Mythic, Mythic+, Timewalking). "Already
covered" (Settings) skips a call-out while a defensive of the set is up.
Not included: timers for small adds, glows from other players' auras on raid
frames (the game hides those auras in Midnight), NSRT's boss-specific assignment
scripts, and a Russian translation. No live WoW test was available in the build
environment: verify encounter reminders in game before you rely on them.
