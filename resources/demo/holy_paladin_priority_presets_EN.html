How to use these priorities

Choose one numbered option

1 is the preferred choice; 2–4 are fallbacks. These are not consecutive casts, and you should not press all four rows. In a two-spell row, both abilities must be usable and relevant.

For example, Party Sustain #1 is Divine Protection + Avenging Wrath. If DP is unavailable, #2 keeps Wings available without requiring an immunity.

Keep personal and party needs separate

Divine Protection and Divine Shield protect you. Aura Mastery with Devotion Aura supplies party mitigation. Avenging Wrath amplifies active healing; it does not prevent a lethal initial hit.

Do not add a party button solely to save a healthy group from a moderate repeat. Use Personal Defensive for that occurrence.

Cooldowns across the route: the reference assumes DP ≈60s, Wings ≈120s, AM ≈180s and Divine Shield ≈300s, with talent effects and observed shorter gaps possible. A new pull does not reset them. A 25–30s repeat cannot receive a fresh DP every time under the 60s assumption. The numbered lists choose among available options; they are not a verified full-route CD schedule. Reserve a party CD for the next boss when the intervening pack is already covered.

Immunity variants

Use Immunity + Party Burst or Immunity + Party Sustain only for a known dangerous personal overlap. Check Forbearance and whether the encounter mechanic respects immunity. A fallback that lacks immunity cannot replace it when immunity is mandatory.

The logs supplied here have no health, death or numerical damage data proving that a particular event requires Divine Shield. Accordingly these variants are supplied as override presets rather than default assignments.
